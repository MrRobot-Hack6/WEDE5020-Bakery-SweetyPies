## Project Overview.
* Bakery called SweetyPies
* History - A small bakery specializing in Boulangerie and Patisserie (Le Cordon Bleu, 2018).
* Mission - They want to provide fresh and delicious baked goods. Get exposure and start being an online business as well.
* Target audience - Busy professionals, families, and health conscious shoppers (Zeely AI, 2025).

## Website Goals and Objectives
The website should increase the number of customers for the bakery.
It is being built specifically for exposure, as the bakery is still small.
It will provide information about the bakery and what they specialize in. 

## Key Features and Functionality.
* Tab section - Homepage, About Us, Contact Us, Enquiries, and Our Services.
* Homepage - The Homepage will include a introduction to the website with an image.
* About Us - This section of the website will explain a brief history and what they do at the bakery.
* Contact Us - Contact details such as email address, cellphone number, and location
* Enquiries - This page has a Google form for customers to fill our for custom orders like custom cakes and baked goods
* Our Services - A few examples of what has been made at the bakery, with prices where they can order a replica of the item.
